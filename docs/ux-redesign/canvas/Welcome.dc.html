Welcome

Get your app ready for a health system's Epic review

Five steps. You describe the app, connect to Epic's sandbox, and the tool tests what Epic will check — sign-in, patient data, writes, security — then gives you a report to send to the health system.

1

Describe your app

About 3 minutes. Who signs in, what kind of app, what it does with patient data, and a few security answers. “Not sure” is fine.

Describe with the assistantFill in the form instead
2

Connect to Epic's sandbox

Register this tool's sign-in address on fhir.epic.com, paste your non-production client ID, sign in once.

3

Run the tests

Only the tests that apply to your app, each with a reason. Takes 1–2 minutes.

4

Resolve what's left

Every result tells you what to do: fix, answer a question, or add proof.

5

Share the report

A PDF the health system's Epic and security reviewers can read on its own.

What you'll need

  • A non-production client ID from fhir.epic.com
  • A sandbox login — a clinician (e.g. FHIRTWO) or a MyChart test patient
  • For clinician apps: a test patient's MRN, e.g. 203713
  • Optional: an answer review key for the security tests

What the result means

“Ready to submit” means your app passes every test the tool can run and you've supplied proof for the rest. It isn't a promise the health system will approve the app — their reviewers make that call.

Everything stays on this computer. No patient data is stored.
