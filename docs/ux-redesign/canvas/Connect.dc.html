Sign-in not working?

  • “redirect_uri is invalid” — the address in step 1 isn't registered exactly (no trailing slash).
  • “invalid_client” — wrong client ID, or Epic hasn't finished activating a new or edited app yet; try again later.
  • Nothing happens — your browser blocked the popup. Allow popups for localhost.
  • Wrong kind of login — clinician apps need a clinician login, not MyChart.

The sign-in is kept in memory only. Restarting the tool means signing in again.
