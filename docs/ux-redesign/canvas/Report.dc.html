2 · Scope

DermScan is a clinician mobile app: a clinician signs in through Epic, searches for and confirms the patient, photographs a lesion, and a dermatologist files a consult note. It reads the problem list, medications and allergies, and sends images to a third-party AI model.

29 tests in scope. Not in scope (5): AUTH-009 and WRITE-001…004 — these apply to patient-facing apps, where the patient is the login.

4 · Method and limits

  • Tests ran against Epic's public sandbox, not Advocate Health's own Epic build; behaviour there may differ.
  • CLIN-005…010 write this tool's own sample photo and note. They show Epic accepts correct writes; they don't prove the app's own code produces them.
  • CLIN-011, CLIN-012 and SEC-001…003 judge the app team's stated answers and attached proof. SEC tests are reviewed by an AI model and every verdict cites the evidence it relied on.
  • Missing information is never counted as a failure; it is reported as “Needs input”.
  • AUTH-007, 008 and 010 test Epic itself and were verified separately with the tool's launch test suite.
